Methodology

How Struxura works

A Struxura engagement doesn't look like a traditional consulting project.
A scope defined before kickoff, an operational deliverable at the end, run directly by the founder.

Engagements with a defined scope

Each module has an indicative duration and a precise deliverable, fixed before it starts. You know what you're getting, and by when.

Before any engagement, three things are aligned with you: the objective, the expected deliverable, and what a satisfactory outcome looks like.

Direct execution or partner pool, depending on scope

Some modules are executed directly by Struxura. Others, which call for specialized technical skills (development, infrastructure, advanced tracking), draw on a pool of selected partners.

Either way, mission oversight, quality assurance and the client relationship stay with Struxura. You have a single point of contact, whatever the module.

A system that keeps working after the engagement

The deliverable of a Struxura engagement keeps working after the engagement ends.

That might be a dashboard the team uses every week. A sales process reps follow without extra coaching. A loyalty infrastructure that keeps collecting data. A configured pipeline that reflects what's actually happening on the ground.

Struxura delivers operational assets: a system that keeps working after the engagement ends.

How to get started

Most Struxura engagements start with a diagnostic module.

STX-01, STX-05, STX-08 or STX-11, depending on the domain: three person-days, a diagnostic deliverable, priced action priorities.

It's a standalone engagement whose outcome belongs to you, whether you continue with Struxura or not.

When the need concerns the whole commercial architecture, the entry point is ARS-01, the structural diagnostic of strategic advisory.
