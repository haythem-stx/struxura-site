The questions worth answering before you build

Answers to the questions founders and executives actually ask before engaging Struxura.

01. Is this for you

When should we run a diagnostic instead of hiring?

Hiring a senior sales profile before running a diagnostic is a costly mistake. A diagnostic first makes sense when:

  • A senior hire hasn't delivered the expected results.
  • Revenue is plateauing despite adding headcount.
  • You're launching a new offer in a new market.
  • Growth depends on one or two people, and you know it.

02. The module catalog

I don't know which module to start with, how do I choose?

The right entry point depends on the real problem, not the catalog. Most engagements start with a diagnostic module: STX-01, STX-05, STX-08 or STX-11, depending on the domain. Three person-days, a diagnostic deliverable, priced action priorities.

If the uncertainty is about the domain itself rather than the module, the module selector (4 questions) or a direct call with Struxura will point you in the right direction, no strings attached.

How does the partner pool work on certain modules?

Some modules are executed directly by Struxura. Others, which call for specialized technical skills (development, infrastructure, advanced tracking), draw on a pool of selected partners. Either way, mission oversight, quality assurance and the client relationship stay with Struxura: you have a single point of contact.

03. Practical questions

Do you only work with Tunisian companies?

No. Struxura works with companies in Tunisia and internationally, with no sector restriction: from launching a new offer to fixing an existing sales system, whenever the diagnostic confirms the engagement is a fit. Engagements are conducted in the client's working language, French or English.

How does the diagnostic module work, and why is it paid?

The diagnostic module is a short, billed engagement that produces a concrete deliverable: an audit or diagnostic report, priced action priorities. It's a standalone engagement whose outcome belongs to you, whether you continue with Struxura or not.

What's the difference between Struxura and a traditional consulting firm?

Ownership: you get an executable operational asset, usable as soon as the engagement ends.
Alignment: you pay for a deliverable defined and scoped before kickoff.
Duration: every module has a start date and an end date announced before it begins.
