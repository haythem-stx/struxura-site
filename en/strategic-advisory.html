Strategic advisory

When the whole commercial architecture needs rebuilding

Some situations reach beyond a single domain. Performance rests on two or three people. Marketing, sales and retention each move on their own. A market shock has shown that the model could not change without breaking.
In these cases, Struxura runs a strategic advisory engagement built on the Adaptive Revenue System (ARS), its proprietary method.

Module or strategic advisory: how to choose

A module addresses a problem located in one specific domain: consumer data, acquisition, measurement, sales organization or pipeline. The scope is short and the deliverable targeted.

Strategic advisory addresses the structure that connects these domains. It is designed for organizations whose growth rests on too few things at once: one person, one channel, one favorable season.

If in doubt, the 30-minute call is there precisely to decide.

Four phases, four separate engagements

The engagement is split into four phases. Each one is contracted separately, with its own duration and deliverable. You decide on the next phase once the deliverable is in hand.

ARS-01 · Diagnose

⏱ 5 person-days, over 2 weeks · mandatory entry point

A structural diagnostic of your commercial architecture: what holds, what depends on a few people, what will break at the next market shift. Deliverable: a written report and a prioritized roadmap.

ARS-02 · Architect

⏱ 15 to 25 person-days, over 6 to 10 weeks

Designing or rebuilding the system: documented customer profiles, a marketing, sales and retention loop aligned on data, early-warning indicators, decision rules. Deliverable: the Revenue Architecture Playbook, a reference document your teams use without Struxura.

ARS-03 · Build

⏱ 10 to 30 person-days, over 4 to 12 weeks

Deploying the architecture. Struxura selects and directs the technical partners, checks what is delivered against the architecture and trains the teams. You keep a single point of contact. Deliverable: an operational, tested system your teams have taken over.

ARS-04 · Transfer

⏱ 3 to 6 months, capped at 6 months

Monitoring of the indicators and coaching of the team that runs the system, with a monthly governance session to spot drift and adjust the structure. The six-month cap is deliberate: at its end, your teams run the system on their own.

What the method rests on

ARS starts from four foundations: what does not change in your clients' buying decision, sensors that signal the model wearing out before it shows in the numbers, commercial components that can be replaced one at a time, and a clear rule for deciding when and how the system must evolve.

Haythem Salhi, founder of Struxura, develops the thinking behind these foundations on haythemsalhi.com.

What you own at the end

A documented architecture, a running system, teams who know how to evolve it. Every deliverable remains yours, whether you continue with Struxura or not.
