Expertise

Five domains. One logic.

Struxura doesn't cover everything. Struxura steps in wherever a business decision is slow to produce results, and where a structured architecture concretely moves the numbers.

Each domain is an operational conviction, not a service category.

1. Loyalty and Consumer Intelligence

"Consumer data is a strategic asset. Not a marketing by-product."

Most organizations accumulate transactions without understanding them. They know their volumes. They don't know their customers.

Struxura builds the infrastructure that turns anonymous transactions into actionable intelligence: who buys, when, why, and what's likely to make them leave.

STX-01 / Consumer Data Audit STX-02 / WhatsApp-First Loyalty Infrastructure STX-03 / In-Pack Code Mechanism STX-04 / Consumer Intelligence Dashboard

Particularly relevant for: any brand with indirect distribution or a high volume of transactions, regardless of sector.

See modules in this domain

2. Acquisition and Demand Generation

"Generating volume without a qualification architecture is a cost. Not an investment."

Many organizations measure acquisition by the number of leads coming in. Few know what their real acquisition cost looks like once it's traced back to a signed contract.

Struxura builds acquisition systems where every dollar of media spend is tracked through to actual conversion: campaign structure, qualification, pipeline, measurement.

STX-05 / Acquisition Performance Audit STX-06 / Acquisition Campaign Architecture STX-07 / Lead Qualification System

Particularly relevant for: any organization with a high volume of leads, regardless of sector.

See modules in this domain

3. Data and Performance Measurement

"What isn't measured can't be managed."

Reporting that reassures without informing is the most dangerous kind. It gives the illusion of control without the means to exercise it.

Struxura installs the analytical foundations that let teams make decisions on real data: reliable sources, structured tracking, indicators tied to actual decisions.

STX-08 / Data Maturity Audit STX-09 / Tracking and Measurement Setup STX-10 / Sales and Marketing Performance Dashboard

Particularly relevant for: any organization with active marketing and sales teams.

See modules in this domain

4. Sales Architecture

"A sales team without architecture sells by luck."

Sales performance isn't a question of individual talent. It's a question of system. When there's no process, results depend on who happens to be having a good month.

Struxura builds the systems that make sales performance repeatable, predictable and transferable: a clear offer, a formalized sales cycle, a structured pipeline.

STX-11 / Sales Flash Diagnostic STX-12 / Offer and Pricing Structuring STX-13 / Pipeline and Sales Cycle Architecture

Particularly relevant for: growing SMEs, companies overhauling their sales operation, teams of 2 to 15 sales reps.

See modules in this domain

5. CRM and Operational Pipeline

"A poorly configured CRM is worse than no CRM at all. It gives the illusion of visibility while hiding the real state of the pipeline."

Most organizations that have invested in a CRM use 20% of its capabilities, with inconsistent data, dead automations and a pipeline that doesn't reflect what's actually happening on the ground.

Struxura puts sales data back in its place: a decision-making tool, not a data-entry chore.

STX-14 / CRM Audit and Restructuring STX-15 / CRM Setup From Scratch STX-16 / B2B Outreach Sequences

Particularly relevant for: any B2B organization with an active sales team.
