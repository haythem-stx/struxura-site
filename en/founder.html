Strategic pivots under real pressure

What twenty years of entrepreneurship teach you about sales systems

Founder and CEO of a multi-brand group built from scratch in a sector still largely offline, B2B operator across international markets from Tunisia. Twenty years of direct accountability for the P&L, the team, the strategy and the outcome.

Three strategic pivots under real pressure: a geopolitical crisis that collapsed the core market in 2014, a post-Covid rebuild in 2022, and in 2025 the choice to refocus the business toward advisory and transferring the method rather than rebuilding the operation once more, made concrete in 2026 with the creation of Struxura Advisory & Services.

Every pivot confirmed the same thing. The problem was never the market. It was always the structure. The system wasn't built to change without breaking.

What Struxura builds for its clients

The convictions behind every Struxura engagement don't come from management theory. They come from a founder who was accountable for the outcome, who built systems under pressure, and who learned what holds and what breaks when the market shifts.

What Struxura builds for its clients is what he wished he'd had before every crisis.
